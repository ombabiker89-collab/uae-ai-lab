# UAE AI services — packaging

## Patterns that sell locally

1. Arabic agent for operations — bilingual, citation-grounded, human approval on writes.
2. Sovereign / resident inference — pitch leads with in-country GPU and export-minimised logs.
3. Domain RAG — Arabic + English policies and contracts. Audit trail is the feature.
4. Dialect adaptation — Jais-class base + Gulf / Sudanese packs.
5. Vision + industrial — sites, yards, energy assets.
6. Eval-as-a-service — Arabic benchmarks, dialect robustness, PDPL red-team.

## Compliance shortlist (not legal advice)

UAE PDPL, sector overlays (health, CBUAE / DFSA / FSRA, government information), free-zone vs mainland invoicing. Do not claim AI Office approval.
