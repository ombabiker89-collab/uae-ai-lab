## Model families

- **Falcon** (TII) — open-weight general and multimodal lines. Start at Falcon-H1 and Falcon-Perception.
- **Jais** — Arabic-first LLMs (MBZUAI + Inception + Cerebras).
- **K2 Think** — MBZUAI reasoning line; marketed as inspectable / sovereign-leaning.
- **Bridge** — `sudanese-dialect-corpus` can fine-tune Jais-class models if the license allows.

## Demand sectors

Government services, healthcare, energy, aviation/ports, finance (DIFC/ADGM), education/Arabic content, smart districts, bilingual support.
